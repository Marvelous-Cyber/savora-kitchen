A kitchen that started at a family table

Savora began with Mama Folake’s Sunday pot in Ikenne, where neighbours stopped by for rice and stayed for the stories. Today we cook the same way: stews fried until the oil floats, rice smoked in the pot and peppers ground fresh each morning.

We buy from local farmers and markets, and we season for flavour first, heat second. Tell us how hot you like it and we will adjust.

Cooked from scratchNo shortcuts on stews, soups or spice blends.
